This guide is for staff pilot testing. Follow normal hotel operations and report anything confusing, blocked, or financially incorrect.

Billing workflow

  1. Open Billing.
  2. Search folio or guest.
  3. Review charges and payments.
  4. Collect or record payment only when verified.
  5. Confirm balance before checkout support.

Payment checks

  1. Paid stays should show zero balance.
  2. Payment due stays must show the correct outstanding amount.
  3. Receipts should be available after payment is recorded.

Exceptions

  1. Do not settle unclear folios without manager approval.
  2. Check tax, room charge, and posted extras before checkout.
  3. Record payment method and reference when available.

Do Not

  • Do not change room status.
  • Do not assign rooms.
  • Do not run front desk check-in unless role access is changed by manager.
