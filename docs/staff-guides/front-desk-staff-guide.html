This guide is for staff pilot testing. Follow normal hotel operations and report anything confusing, blocked, or financially incorrect.

Start of shift

  1. Open Front Desk dashboard.
  2. Review Action Queue first.
  3. Check arrivals, departures, rooms to clean, and guests in house.

New booking

  1. Click New Booking.
  2. Create or select guest.
  3. Pick stay dates, room type, guests, and payment plan.
  4. Create booking, then assign room when ready.

Assign room

  1. Click Assign Room.
  2. Only ready rooms are shown.
  3. Choose a guest from eligible reservations.
  4. After assignment, continue to check-in.

Check-in

  1. Verify identity.
  2. Complete missing guest details.
  3. Review payment plan.
  4. Confirm room is ready.
  5. Complete check-in.

Checkout

  1. Open Check Out.
  2. Open due departure.
  3. Collect any balance before final checkout.
  4. Complete checkout only after billing is settled.

If something is blocked

  1. Read the message first. Most blocked actions explain the missing step.
  2. If a room is not shown, it is usually not ready, already occupied, assigned, or incompatible.
  3. If checkout is blocked, collect or settle the outstanding balance first.
  4. If the guest details step does not clear, check address, country, state, city, purpose of visit, mobile, and nationality.

What to report during pilot

  1. Wrong count on dashboard cards.
  2. Eligible reservation not visible in Assign Guest.
  3. Wrong balance or paid booking showing due.
  4. Check-in or checkout button disabled without a clear reason.
  5. Any screen where staff cannot understand the next action.

Do Not

  • Do not use Billing or Settings directly unless manager gives access.
  • Do not checkout with an unpaid balance.
  • Do not assign rooms outside the ready-room flow unless needed.
